How It Works

From humming a tune
to singing in harmony

Three simple steps. No music theory. No sheet music. Just sing.

01
🎤
Sing your song
Hold up your phone and sing any song you love — a classic, a lullaby, a pop hit, anything. HarmonyAlong listens and understands your melody.
02
🎵
Choose your parts
Pick how many voices you want — two parts for a duet, three for a trio, up to five for a full ensemble. The app creates each harmony part automatically.
03
🎶
Learn & sing together
Toggle each part on and off. Practice your part alone first, then bring in the others. When everyone's ready — sing it together.

Features

Everything you need
to sound beautiful together

Built for people who love music, not people who studied it.

🎤
Sing Into Your Phone
No instruments needed. Just sing any song directly into HarmonyAlong and it figures out the rest.
🎚️
Toggle Parts On & Off
Hear your part alone, or blend it with others. Practice at your own pace until it feels natural.
🔢
2 to 5 Part Harmony
A duet for two or a full five-part arrangement for your whole group — you choose how many voices.
🤝
Share With Your Group
Send your arrangement to everyone who's singing. Each person learns their own part, then you come together.
⭐
Community Ratings
Love an arrangement? Rate it. The community helps train the AI to get harmonies even more beautiful over time.
🎵
Any Song, Any Style
Pop, gospel, folk, holiday classics, lullabies — if you can sing it, HarmonyAlong can harmonize it.
