A Walkthrough

How HarmonyAlong
actually works.

Five simple steps. No music training required.

1

Sing a song you love

Tap the microphone and sing any song you love — a verse and a chorus is plenty. Don't worry about being perfect. Auto-tune gently corrects your pitch in real time as the app listens, the same way professional singers use it on stage.

2

AI listens & creates harmonies

Behind the scenes, the app detects your melody, identifies the underlying chords, and generates singable harmony parts based on the style and number of voices you've chosen — from a simple two-part duet to a rich five-voice arrangement.

3

Choose your style

Pick the vibe — pop, gospel, folk — and HarmonyAlong shapes the harmonies to match.

4

Practice your part — alone or together

Each singer picks their part. Tap a singer to mute or unmute their voice. Slide each volume up or down. Practice your part alone first — then bring the others back in until you can hold your own against the full harmony.

5

Sing it together

When everyone knows their part, gather the people you love. Press play. Sing it together. That's the moment HarmonyAlong was built for.

Common Questions

Things you're probably wondering.

Have a different question? Reach out to us at harmonyalongapp@gmail.com

Do I need to know how to sing well? +

Not at all. HarmonyAlong is built for everyday singers — from people who just love singing in the shower to those who sing in choirs. The app uses auto-tune to gently correct pitch as it listens, so don't worry about hitting every note perfectly.

Do I need to know how to read music? +

Nope. HarmonyAlong is designed for people who don't read music. Each harmony part is taught the way singers naturally learn — by listening. Just press play and follow along with your voice.

When will the app launch? +

We're in early development. Sign up for the waitlist and you'll be among the first to know — and the first to try it. Waitlist members will get priority access before the public launch.

Will it be free? +

There will be a free version so anyone can experience the magic of harmonizing with people they love. Premium features will be offered for those who want unlimited use, advanced controls, and additional features.

What kind of songs work? +

Any song you can sing — pop, gospel, folk, lullabies, hymns, holiday classics, musical theater, songs in any language. If you can hum it, HarmonyAlong can harmonize it.

How is my voice data handled? +

Your privacy matters to us. Your voice will be used only to detect the melody — it won't be stored, sold, or used to train other AI systems. Full details on our privacy approach will be shared at launch.

Will it work on my phone? +

HarmonyAlong is being built for both iPhone and Android, with web access coming later. Tell us which device you use when you sign up so we can prioritize your platform.
